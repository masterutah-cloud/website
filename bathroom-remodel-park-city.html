FAQ

Common questions.

How long does a bathroom remodel take?+
Most single-bathroom remodels are completed in about three weeks once work starts. Larger primary suites or layout changes can take longer; your quote includes a written schedule.
Do I need a permit?+
Permits are usually required when plumbing, electrical or walls are moved. We handle the permit process with the city or county as part of the project.
Can we stay in the house during the remodel?+
Yes, in most cases, as long as you have another working bathroom. We seal off the work area to contain dust and clean up at the end of each day.
