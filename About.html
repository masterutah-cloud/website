Our story

A different kind of crew.

Master Construction Group was founded on a simple idea: the homeowner deserves to know what's happening on their home every day, the work should be done right the first time, and the schedule we commit to is the schedule we follow closely.

10+ years and 400+ completed projects later, that's still how we run every job — from a guest-bath refresh in Old Town to a top-to-bottom whole-home remodel on the Promontory ridge.

We treat every project like a rough diamond, we cut, shape, and finish until its best comes through. When you call, you reach the people building your home.

What we hold ourselves to

Four standards. Every project.

i.

Done right the first time

The right materials, the right methods, the right sequence —  We build to last decades, not seasons.

ii.

Communication daily

Weekly progress photos, daily updates, a clean job site at the end of the day. You know what's happening before you ask.

iii.

Schedules we keep

The timeline in the proposal is the timeline we deliver. If we miss a milestone, you hear from us directly.

iv.

Owner-led, every project

The person you meet at the consultation is the person on the job site. No middlemen, no sales reps, no surprises.
