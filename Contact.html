Before you call

A few quick answers.

The most common questions we get before a first conversation.

How quickly will I hear back?+
We respond to every inquiry within twenty-four hours, usually the same business day. After the consultation, expect your full proposal within five business days.
Is the consultation really free?+
Yes. The first walkthrough, ballpark numbers, and material guidance are free and carry no obligation. We only charge once you've signed a written proposal.
What information should I prepare?+
A few photos of the space, a rough idea of your timeline and budget, and any inspiration images you've been collecting. None of it has to be polished — we'll take it from there.
Do you work with our designer or architect?+
Yes. We collaborate with several interior designers and architects across the Park City area, and we're happy to step into your existing team. If you don't have one, we can recommend the right partner for your project.
