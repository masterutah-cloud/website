Project Spotlight: A Kitchen Backsplash in Hideout

Blog — March 18, 2026

The brief

This Hideout kitchen needed a full rebuild around a household that actually cooks daily — not a kitchen designed to photograph well and sit unused. Custom cabinetry, integrated appliances and full-slab stone counters set the framework; tile carried the visual weight behind the range.

The tile work

A full-height large-format porcelain backsplash runs behind the range and up to the hood surround, matched in tone to the waterfall-edge island stone so the two materials read as one continuous palette rather than competing finishes.

Why it holds up daily

Large-format tile behind a range means fewer grout lines to catch grease and splatter — a practical choice for a kitchen that's used, not staged.
